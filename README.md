 **The Project Development**
 
PRX STUDIOS

Semi-Open World Backrooms Survival Game.

“If you’re not careful and you noclip out of reality in the wrong areas, you’ll end up in the Backrooms, where it’s nothing but the stink of old moist carpet, the madness of mono-yellow, the endless background noise of fluorescent lights at maximum hum-buzz and approximately six hundred million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms to be trapped in. God save you if you hear something wandering around nearby, because it sure as hell has heard you.”

--------------------------------------------

 **Game Vision:** 
 
 Presistent Server based Backrooms survival game with emphasis on realism, nostalgia, expansive exploration, and horror. Our notable influences include, Ark: Survival Evolved, Rust, and Escape The Backrooms.
 
--------------------------------------------

 This Repository will hold all the information related to the game including but not limited to, game design, lore, environmental style guides, monster design and functionality, etc.

 --------------------------------------------
</div>

 **Developmental Goals**

 - Create a game players can come back to.
 - Emulate the feeling, and Aliveness of The Backrooms to the best of our ability.
 - Contrasting gameplay that fluctuates from being fun and slightly competitive to horror.
 - Monsters and Entities designed past the usual generic pathfinding to kill, into something more real.
 - Players should feel like they're interacting with the environments, not just viewing them from the surface.
